📖 Transparent Accounting Formulas

1. Sales Revenue:

Sum of all active customer sales recorded during the selected period. Cancelled sales are fully excluded.

2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):

COGS = Quantity Sold × Weighted Average Cost (WAC) per egg at sale

3. Operating Expenses:

Day-to-day depot costs including transport, packaging paper trays, depot electricity, and labour.

4. Egg Losses Cost:

Loss Cost = Broken / Spoiled Eggs × WAC Unit Cost. Accounted for once to prevent double-deduction.

5. Net Business Profit:

Net Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses − Loss Cost
